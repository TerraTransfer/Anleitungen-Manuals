Description

The Aquatos Radar Type 0470 measures the distance to the water surface with a 60 GHz radar signal (pulsed coherent radar) and converts it into the water level. A dielectric radar lens focuses the beam to approx. 10°, so walls and fittings in shafts or under bridges do not disturb the measurement.

Up to three reflections are captured simultaneously, each with signal strength in dB. Spurious echoes can thus be identified and suppressed via measuring range, sensitivity and sorting. Commissioning is done on site with the Bluetooth app in the browser, with a raw scan for alignment and a live plot. Optionally the radar can be combined online with the Aquatos Web LTX and operated via the Sensormanager web portal.

Applications
  • Gauging stations on streams, rivers and canals, flood early warning
  • Storm overflows and relief structures, also under closed manhole covers
  • Retention basins, tanks, waters with ice drift
